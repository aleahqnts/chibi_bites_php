What is Mochi?


At Chibi Bites, mochi is our soft and chewy rice treat that’s perfect for any sweet craving.

Made from glutinous rice, each bite has a unique, chewy texture and a gentle sweetness that melts in your mouth. Our mochis come in a variety of flavors, making them the perfect snack or dessert for everyone.

Berry Cream

A soft, chewy mochi filled with smooth, creamy goodness and a burst of sweet berry flavor. Perfectly balanced between fruity and creamy, this best-seller is light, refreshing, and irresistibly satisfying in every bite.

Matcha Zen

Delicately chewy mochi infused with rich matcha flavor and a creamy center that melts in your mouth. Earthy, smooth, and calming, this best-seller is perfect for matcha lovers seeking a peaceful, not-too-sweet treat.
