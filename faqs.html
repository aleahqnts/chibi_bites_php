FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How should I store Chibi Bites?
Chibi Bites can be stored at room temperature. For best freshness and quality, refrigeration is recommended.
What is the shelf life?
Chibi Bites have a shelf life of up to 3 days when refrigerated. If kept at room temperature, we recommend consuming them within the day.
Can I place advance orders?
Yes. Advance orders are highly recommended, particularly for bulk or event orders, to ensure availability.
How does delivery work?
All orders are freshly prepared. Some may be delivered chilled to help maintain quality during transit. Refrigeration after receiving is optional but advised.
How should I consume Chibi Bites?
Chibi Bites can be enjoyed at room temperature. If refrigerated, allow a few minutes for a softer texture.
Allergen Information
Chibi Bites are made with glutinous rice flour, which is naturally gluten-free. Some ingredients may contain dairy, soy, and wheat (gluten). Customers with food allergies are advised to exercise caution or contact us before ordering.

Please feel free to message us, we’re happy to assist.
